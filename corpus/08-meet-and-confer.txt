On behalf of Hartley Manufacturing Inc., we advise you of deficiencies in the discovery responses to our client's First Set of Interrogatories and First Request for Production of Documents, served June 18, 2026.

Discovery disputes are best resolved through good-faith discussions under Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(f) and the Court's Scheduling Order of July 1, 2026. We seek to resolve these deficiencies without Court intervention, but reserve all rights available to our client.

SPECIFIC DEFICIENCIES

1. Interrogatory No. 3: Your client's response promises "reasonable efforts" without a specific completion date. The response must be complete and accurate as required by Fed. R. Civ. P. 33(a)(2).

2. Interrogatory Nos. 7 and 8: Your client objects as "overly broad and burdensome" without factual support. Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(b)(1), proportional discovery is required unless protected.

3. Request for Production No. 5: Your response stating "not applicable" contradicts the clear request for all communications with customers (January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2025). This is non-responsive.

4. Request for Production No. 12: You withheld 312 pages claiming attorney-client privilege but provided no privilege log. Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(b)(5) requires a privilege log with document descriptions, dates, parties, and legal basis.

5. Request for Production No. 18: Only 23 invoices were produced for the Portland territory, yet hundreds more appear to exist. A thorough search in good faith is required.

Your client must supplement discovery responses when additional information is discovered, per Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(e).

PROPOSAL TO CONFER

We propose that counsel meet (in person or via videoconference) on or before August 5, 2026, to discuss these deficiencies and reach agreement on supplemented responses. Please confirm your availability.

If these disputes are not resolved through good-faith discussion, our client will file a Motion to Compel pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(a), seeking reasonable expenses and attorneys' fees.
