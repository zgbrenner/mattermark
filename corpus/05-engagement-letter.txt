RE: Engagement Letter - Intellectual Property Licensing Matter

Dear Mr. Foster:

This letter confirms that Harrison & Cole LLP (the "Firm") agrees to represent Bright Futures Technology Inc. (the "Client") regarding the intellectual property licensing dispute with Nexus Digital Partners, LLC. This engagement letter sets forth the terms and conditions of our representation.

SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT

The Firm will provide legal services related to the negotiation, drafting, and review of intellectual property licensing agreements. Our services shall include:

- Analysis of existing licensing arrangements and intellectual property rights
- Negotiation of license terms with Nexus Digital Partners, LLC
- Drafting and revision of license documentation
- Legal opinions regarding enforceability of licensing provisions
- General counsel on intellectual property matters related to this engagement

The Client agrees that this engagement is limited to the matters described above. Any additional legal services will be the subject of separate engagement or modification of this letter.

RETAINER

To secure our representation, the Client agrees to deposit a retainer of Twenty-Five Thousand Dollars ($25,000.00) with the Firm within five (5) business days of execution of this letter. This retainer will be held in a trust account and applied against invoices as services are rendered. The Client will replenish the retainer upon written request from the Firm when the balance falls below Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000.00). Upon completion of the matter or termination of this engagement, any unused retainer shall be returned to the Client within thirty (30) days.

CONFLICTS OF INTEREST AND CONSENT

The Client warrants that it has disclosed all known conflicts of interest to the Firm. The Firm represents that it has no material conflicts with respect to this engagement. However, the Firm may represent other clients in matters that do not involve Nexus Digital Partners, LLC or the Client's intellectual property.

By signing below, the Client waives any conflicts that the Firm has disclosed and consents to the Firm's continued representation of other clients in unrelated matters. The Firm will maintain strict confidentiality between matters and will implement appropriate screening procedures to prevent disclosure of confidential information.

TERMINATION AND CONCLUSIONS

Either party may terminate this engagement upon written notice. Upon termination, the Client shall pay all outstanding invoices within thirty (30) days. The Firm shall transfer the file and work product to successor counsel upon Client's request.

By executing this letter, both parties agree to the terms and conditions stated herein. This letter shall remain in effect until terminated in writing.
