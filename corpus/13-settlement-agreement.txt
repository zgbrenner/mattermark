CONFIDENTIAL SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND MUTUAL RELEASE

This Confidential Settlement Agreement and Mutual Release (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of April 14, 2026, by and between Halvorsen Millwork & Supply Co., an Oregon corporation ("Plaintiff"), and Birchgate Logistics Partners, Inc., a Nevada corporation ("Defendant"). Plaintiff and Defendant are each referred to herein as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Plaintiff filed an action captioned Halvorsen Millwork & Supply Co. v. Birchgate Logistics Partners, Inc., Case No. 26-CV-01847, in the Circuit Court of Marion County, Oregon (the "Action"), alleging breach of contract and related claims arising from a freight and warehousing agreement dated January 8, 2025;

WHEREAS, Defendant has denied and continues to deny any liability or wrongdoing whatsoever with respect to the claims asserted in the Action;

WHEREAS, the Parties participated in a mediation session on March 20, 2026, before mediator Ines P. Callahan, and reached agreement in principle on the terms of a full and final resolution of the Action; and

WHEREAS, the Parties now desire to settle and resolve, fully and finally, any and all disputes between them, including but not limited to those raised or that could have been raised in the Action, on the terms set forth below;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants, and releases contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows.

1. Settlement Payment. Defendant shall pay Plaintiff the total sum of Two Hundred Ten Thousand Dollars ($210,000.00) (the "Settlement Amount") according to the installment schedule below. Payments shall be made by wire transfer to the trust account of Plaintiff's counsel, Oduya & Ferris LLP, per the wiring instructions to be provided separately.

    Installment      Amount           Due Date
    ---------------  ---------------  -----------------
    First            $70,000.00       May 1, 2026
    Second            $70,000.00       July 1, 2026
    Third              $70,000.00       September 1, 2026

    Total             $210,000.00

2. Late Payment. If any installment is not received within ten (10) business days after its due date, Defendant shall have five (5) additional business days after written notice from Plaintiff's counsel to cure the default before any remaining unpaid balance becomes immediately due and payable in full, together with interest accruing at eight percent (8%) per annum from the date of default.

3. Mutual Release. Except for the obligations created by this Agreement, each Party, on behalf of itself and its respective predecessors, successors, assigns, officers, directors, employees, and agents, hereby releases and forever discharges the other Party and its respective predecessors, successors, assigns, officers, directors, employees, and agents from any and all claims, demands, causes of action, damages, and liabilities of any kind, whether known or unknown, arising out of or related to the Action or the freight and warehousing agreement dated January 8, 2025, from the beginning of time through the date of this Agreement.

4. Waiver of Unknown Claims. Each Party acknowledges that it may later discover facts different from or in addition to those it now knows or believes to be true with respect to the matters released herein, and each Party nonetheless agrees that the release in Paragraph 3 shall remain effective in all respects notwithstanding any such different or additional facts.

5. Dismissal with Prejudice. Within five (5) business days after receipt of the first installment identified in Paragraph 1, the Parties shall file a stipulated dismissal of the Action with prejudice, with each Party to bear its own costs and attorneys' fees except as otherwise provided herein. Plaintiff's counsel shall be responsible for preparing and filing the stipulation of dismissal.

6. No Admission of Liability. This Agreement reflects the compromise of disputed claims and shall not be construed as an admission by either Party of any liability, fault, or wrongdoing of any kind. Neither this Agreement nor any of its terms shall be offered or admitted into evidence in any proceeding, except a proceeding to enforce the terms of this Agreement itself.

7. Confidentiality. The Parties agree that the terms of this Agreement, including the Settlement Amount, shall remain strictly confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party, except (a) to each Party's attorneys, accountants, and financial advisors on a need-to-know basis, (b) as required by subpoena, court order, or applicable law, or (c) as necessary to enforce the terms of this Agreement. Any Party compelled to disclose the terms of this Agreement shall provide prompt written notice to the other Party prior to disclosure, to the extent legally permitted.

8. Non-Disparagement. Each Party agrees that it shall not, and shall direct its officers, directors, and employees not to, make any disparaging, defamatory, or negative statements, whether oral or written, regarding the other Party, its products, services, officers, directors, or employees, provided that nothing in this Paragraph 8 shall prohibit truthful testimony given under oath or statements required by law.

9. No Further Litigation. Each Party represents that it has not filed, and covenants that it will not file, any further complaint, charge, or lawsuit against the other Party with respect to any claim released under Paragraph 3, except an action to enforce this Agreement.

10. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Oregon, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

11. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, understandings, and agreements, whether written or oral. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

12. Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

13. Authority. Each signatory represents and warrants that he or she has full authority to execute this Agreement on behalf of the Party for whom he or she signs.

14. Counterparts. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
